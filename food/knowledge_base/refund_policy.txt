Refund and Cancellation Policy - Spice Route Kitchen

Refunds: If a delivered order is incorrect or damaged, report it within 2 hours of delivery. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.
Cancelled orders: A cancelled order is refunded in full when cancelled before preparation starts.
Quality issues: Please contact the restaurant with the order number so the team can review the issue.
Restaurant staff never ask for passwords or card PINs over chat.
