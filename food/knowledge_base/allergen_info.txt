Allergen and Dietary Information - Spice Route Kitchen

Vegetarian dishes are marked as vegetarian on the menu. Vegetarian dishes never contain meat, fish, or egg.
Nut allergens: Our kitchen prepares dishes that may contain traces of peanuts and cashews. Customers with nut allergies should contact the restaurant before ordering.
Gluten: Naan and fried rice contain gluten. We do not offer a certified gluten-free menu.
Spice level: Dishes are prepared with medium spice by default. Please mention spice preferences when calling the restaurant.
Restaurant opening hours: The kitchen is open from 10:00 to 23:00 every day, and the menu is updated every Monday.
